Popular apartments in Iowa City

These are the buildings students review the most and rate the highest on LeaseLens. Popularity is not the whole story, so each listing includes what tenants love and what they complain about.

How we decide what is popular

We rank buildings by a blend of total verified reviews, average overall rating, and how many tenants say they would renew. A building needs at least 200 verified reviews from the past three years to appear on this list. Rents shown are the monthly cost per bedroom in the current leasing cycle.

The top five

1. Maplewren Flats

4.7 / 5 412 reviews

Downtown • $895 to $1,150 per bedroom

Tenants love: A five-minute walk to the Pentacrest, fast maintenance replies, and a rooftop study lounge.

Tenants mention: Weekend noise from nearby bars on the east side of the building.

2. The Cardinal Lofts

4.5 / 5 368 reviews

Downtown • $950 to $1,225 per bedroom

Tenants love: High ceilings, in-unit laundry, and a front desk that signs for packages.

Tenants mention: Parking costs extra and spaces fill up quickly.

3. Foxglove Terrace

4.4 / 5 301 reviews

Near Southside • $780 to $980 per bedroom

Tenants love: Spacious bedrooms, a quiet courtyard, and a manager who knows tenants by name.

Tenants mention: Older appliances and slow internet at peak evening hours.

4. Prairie Lantern Apartments

4.3 / 5 275 reviews

Northside • $690 to $850 per bedroom

Tenants love: Reasonable rent, free parking, and a laid-back neighborhood.

Tenants mention: A 15-minute walk to central campus, so most residents use the bus.

5. Ironwood Yard

4.2 / 5 240 reviews

Near Eastside • $825 to $1,050 per bedroom

Tenants love: A fitness room, bike storage, and a pet-friendly policy.

Tenants mention: Move-out charges were higher than some tenants expected.

Iowa City apartments downtown

Two of our top five Iowa City apartments downtown, Maplewren Flats and The Cardinal Lofts, rate 4.5 or higher. Maplewren Flats is a five-minute walk from the Pentacrest. The Cardinal Lofts adds in-unit laundry and a front desk, though parking costs extra.

Why popular does not always mean right for you

A building can be popular because of its location, but a downtown address may not fit if you want quiet evenings. Read the most recent reviews first, and look at the category scores instead of only the overall number. If noise matters to you, check the noise score. If you are watching your budget, compare the value-for-rent score.
