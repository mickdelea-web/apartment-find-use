LeaseLens is a free rating app with Iowa City apartment reviews written by the college students who live there. Real tenants rate noise, maintenance, and landlord responsiveness so you can compare Iowa City apartments and sign a lease with confidence.

See popular Iowa City apartments

How LeaseLens works

1. Verified tenants only

To post a review, you upload a lease page or a utility bill with your address. We check it and then delete the file. Every rating comes from someone who has lived there.

2. Ratings that matter to students

Each building is scored from 1 to 5 on walkability to campus, noise, maintenance speed, value for rent, and roommate friendliness.

3. Compare and decide

Filter by price, bedroom count, and distance to the Pentacrest. Save favorites and share a shortlist with your future roommates.

Our promise on ratings

Landlords cannot pay to change a score or hide a review. Property managers can reply publicly to any review, and we mark replies clearly so readers see both sides. Scores are averaged from the last three years of verified tenant reviews, so an old problem that has been fixed does not follow a building forever.
