Cheap apartments in Iowa City

Low rent should not mean a bad experience. These budget-friendly buildings earned fair ratings from verified student tenants, and we explain the extra costs that can turn a bargain into a surprise.

Every building below has a monthly rent per bedroom of $740 or less at its lowest price tier and an overall rating of at least 3.9 from verified tenants.

Hidden costs to watch for

The advertised rent is rarely the full monthly cost. Before you sign, ask about each of these:

  • Utilities: Electric and internet can add $60 to $120 per person each month in winter.
  • Parking: Some buildings charge $25 to $75 per month for a space.
  • Fees: Application, pet, and amenity fees add up. Ask for a full list in writing.
  • Deposits: Ask how much of the deposit tenants usually get back, and read the move-out rules.
  • Renter's insurance: Some landlords require it, and you should have it even if they do not.

Ways to lower your rent

Add a roommate

Moving from a two-bedroom to a four-bedroom unit often drops the per-person price by $100 or more.

Live farther out

Each mile away from downtown can save you money. Check the bus route first so the savings are not eaten by gas and parking.

Ask about move-in specials

Spring and summer are common times for waived fees or a free month, especially in less popular buildings.

Sublease for a semester

If you only need housing for part of the year, a sublease can be cheaper than a full lease. Get landlord approval in writing.

A sample monthly budget

Say you choose Copperline Court at $640 per bedroom. Add about $55 for electricity, $30 for internet, $25 for parking, and $15 for renter's insurance, and your true cost is closer to $765. Comparing the total, not just the rent, is the best way to tell which cheap apartment is truly the cheapest.
